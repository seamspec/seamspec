# Code of conduct

SeamSpec follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). In short: be welcoming and respectful, criticise ideas rather than people, and leave out harassment of any kind. This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and anywhere else you represent the project.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour bothers you, tell the maintainers privately through the [Security tab](https://github.com/seamspec/seamspec/security): choose **Report a vulnerability** and say it's a conduct report. Reports are kept confidential.

The maintainers decide on a response, from a private word to a ban, following the Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
